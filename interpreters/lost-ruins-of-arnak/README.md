# Lost Ruins of Arnak Rules Interpreter

Version `0.1.0` is a bounded, original-interpretation play aid based on the uploaded 24-page English Lost Ruins of Arnak game manual/rulebook. It is not a reproduction of the manual, card text, site text, guardian text, artwork, or other component text. The PDF is quarantined in the source-audit package and is not bundled here.

## Coverage

The corpus covers the document-supported structure of setup, five-round play, one-main-action turns, free actions, worker placement and site discovery, high-level resource/card economy, guardians, the research track, assistants, passing and round cleanup, and final scoring boundaries. It deliberately avoids exact card, site, guardian, assistant, research-bonus, temple-tile, and other component effects. Those questions must use an authorized current reference or return `not_yet_covered`.

## Edition and catalogue boundary

- Catalogue status: **out-of-snapshot candidate**; Lost Ruins of Arnak is not present in the fixed top-50 identity snapshot.
- Working identity: likely base game, likely English, 24-page game manual/rulebook; the document presents Bird Temple as the first-game side and includes the Snake Temple side variant.
- Edition scope: document scope only. Exact printing, errata state, language edition, and publisher provenance remain unresolved.
- Confidence describes the interpretation's support in this artifact; it is not a claim that all exceptions are covered.
